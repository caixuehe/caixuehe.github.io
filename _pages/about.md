Hi, I'm Caixue. 

I'm a Master's student at [Capital Normal University](https://english.cnu.edu.cn/) and a Research Assistant at [HKUST(GZ) AI Thrust](https://hkust-gz.edu.cn/). My research lives at the intersection of large language models and people who use them.

I currently work on two questions:

1. **Can we edit what a large language model "knows" without breaking what it does well?** This is the model knowledge editing problem — small, targeted updates to a model's factual beliefs, without retraining the whole thing.
2. **Can language models be a useful supporting voice for mental health, instead of a risky replacement for it?** I build and evaluate dialogue systems for mental health support, with a focus on safety, empathy, and respecting professional boundaries.

If either of those resonate with you — for collaboration, paper discussion, or just a chat — please [email me](mailto:caixuehe@hkust-gz.edu.cn).
